The task is to solve a Tower of Hanoi problem. There are disks of different sizes that must be stacked in a specific order: largest disk at the bottom, smallest at the top. All disks must be placed within the target zone on the table.

The place action requires an explicit z parameter: place([obj], {'x': ..., 'y': ..., 'z': ..., 'theta': ...}).

Key constraints:
- Stack the disks in size order: largest (bottom) to smallest (top).
- The largest disk goes on the table surface first (z ≈ 0.01-0.02).
- Each subsequent disk must be smaller than the one below it.
- Compute z for each disk as the cumulative height of all disks already placed.
- All disks must share the same (x, y) center — use the target zone center.
- A larger disk must NEVER be placed on top of a smaller disk.
- Theta should be 0 for all placements (disks are symmetric).

Goal: All disks stacked largest-to-smallest (bottom-to-top), centered in the target zone.
