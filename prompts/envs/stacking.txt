The tabletop environment contains a robot arm, a marked target zone, and several
objects that must be stacked in a specified bottom-to-top order. The robot sits
at (0, 0), faces positive x, and positive z points upward.

Available primitive actions:
- pick([obj], {}): pick an object from the table or from the top of a partial stack.
- place([obj], {"x": ..., "y": ..., "z": ..., "theta": ...}): place the held
  object at a 3D pose. A support object must be placed before an object that is
  required above it. The environment validates support and exact stack order.

Use the target-zone center for the base. Place each later object on the center and
top surface of its required predecessor. Do not place an upper object before its
support exists.
